== Evidence interpretation

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
| Topic | Supported interpretation | Required evidence

| Endpoint access
| Only callers allowed to `get services` in the namespace reach the guardrails server.
| `401` anonymous, `200` own token, `403` another tenant's token (Module 1, Exercise 3).

| Pattern rails
| Regex and Presidio block what their patterns name, in milliseconds, and nothing else.
| Module 1 verdicts and `regex-only` recall 0.00 on the labelled set.

| Content-safety rail
| On this set, the model blocks all unsafe prompts and refuses about one legitimate prompt in ten.
| `content-safety` TP=20 FN=0 FP=3 TN=27, with the notebook and monitor tallies agreeing.

| Policy tuning
| On this set, removing `S9: PII/Privacy` from Nemotron Safety Guard's prompt removed that category from the model's answers, and changed a verdict only where no other category fired. Another edit can change labels, verdicts, both or neither: measure it.
| `tuned` FP 3 to 2: "social security number" allowed, the gmail password prompt still blocked as criminal planning.

| Outage behaviour
| An unreachable safety model yields no verdict; the client's error handling decides fail-open or fail-closed.
| `ERROR` verdicts, the pod log's connection error, and the two outage runs.

| LLM judge
| A prompted LLM judge can match or beat the fine-tuned model on this set, but only once its answers fit NeMo's parser, at about three times the latency; its verdicts vary between runs by about as much as a policy edit changes them.
| The naive run's false positives next to `explain()` showing the judge answered *No*; the 50-prompt comparison table; the moved prompts after the policy edit, each asked three more times (Module 5).
|===

== What this lab does not prove

* *Production accuracy.* 50 prompts, 40 of them from a dataset the model was trained on, give directional results only. Evaluate on your own traffic.
* *Output rails.* Only input screening is measured; checking a model's answers is not exercised.
* *Multilingual coverage.* The set is English. The reference workshop shows weaker results on Korean.
* *Latency at scale.* The safety model runs quantized on CPU and is shared by everyone in the class; the LLM judge is a remote endpoint shared beyond it.
* *Judge stability.* A reasoning model's verdicts on borderline prompts can change between runs, even at temperature 0.
* *Robustness to adversarial prompts.* Jailbreak and obfuscation attacks are out of scope.
